Privacy Policy for Vaultly

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Vaultly is a personal finance app built to keep your financial data private. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

Vaultly does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on a server. Everything you enter transactions, budgets, categories, and your account name stays on your device, inside the app's local storage. There is no backend, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party data sharing of any kind.

What data is stored, and where

Vaultly stores the following information locally on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Transactions (amount, category, date, note)
  • Budgets (limit, category, time period)
  • Your chosen currency and account name

None of this data ever leaves your device unless you personally choose to export it. We do not have servers, and we cannot see, access, or recover this data it exists only on your device.

Face ID / Touch ID

Vaultly uses Face ID or Touch ID (via Apple's LocalAuthentication framework) to lock the app. When you unlock Vaultly, your device's Secure Enclave verifies your identity directly, Vaultly itself never receives, sees, or stores your biometric data. The app only ever receives a yes/no result: authentication succeeded or failed.

Exporting your data

Vaultly lets you export your transactions as a CSV file from Settings. This file is created locally and only leaves your device if you choose to share it yourself. We have no visibility into, or control over, what you do with an exported file.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete All Data. This immediately and irreversibly removes your transactions, budgets, and account information from your device.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any of your data to any third party, because we do not have access to it in the first place. Vaultly makes no network requests and includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Vaultly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
